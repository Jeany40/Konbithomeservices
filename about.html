What our name means

Men anpil, chay pa lou.

Many hands, light work.

Konbit comes from Haitian Creole. In Haiti, a konbit is a tradition of people coming together to finish a big job, like neighbors working a field side by side until the work is done. The proverb says it plainly: with many hands, the load is light. It’s how we work as a family, on every job.
