01Bathroom

Tub-to-Shower Bathroom Renovation

This bathroom had an old tub with frosted sliding doors and worn tile. To turn it into a walk-in shower, we took the space down to the studs and built the shower back from the walls in.

Bathroom renovationsTile installationRequest a similar project

Before
A dated bathtub with frosted sliding doors, worn wall tile, and an over-the-toilet cabinet.
The goal
Replace the tub with a walk-in shower.
The work
We removed the tub, tile, and walls down to the studs. We covered the shower walls with GoBoard, a waterproof foam backer board, and built a new shower base and curb around the drain. Then we set large marble-look tile with a leveling system, built in a recessed niche, and laid a hexagon mosaic floor.
The result
A walk-in shower with large gray marble-look tile, a hexagon mosaic floor, a built-in niche, black rain and handheld shower fixtures, and a sliding glass door.

Work stages

  1. Stage 1

    Down to the studs

    The old tub, tile, and walls came out, down to the framing.

  2. Stage 2

    Backer board and shower base

    GoBoard waterproof backer board on the shower walls, and a new shower base and curb around the drain.

  3. Stage 3

    Tile, set level

    Large wall tile set with leveling clips, with the niche framed in.

02Bathroom

New Bathroom, Framing to Finish

We built this bathroom from bare stud walls: framing, backer board, a tiled walk-in shower, floor tile, the vanity, and the fixtures.

Bathroom renovationsFraming & drywallTile installationRequest a similar project

Before
Bare framing: wood stud walls in front of foil-faced foam insulation board, with the pipes and wiring roughed in.
The goal
A complete new bathroom with a walk-in shower.
The work
We framed the walls, put GoBoard waterproof backer board on the shower walls and green mold-resistant drywall on the rest of the room, and built the shower base and curb. We tiled the shower in large marble-look tile with a hexagon mosaic floor and a niche with a hexagon tile back, then set large marble-look floor tile with a leveling system.
The result
A finished bathroom with a tiled walk-in shower, a black-framed sliding glass door, black fixtures, a gray shaker vanity, and a backlit mirror.

The finished room

  2. Stage 3

    Large marble-look wall tile, a hexagon mosaic floor, and a built-in niche.

  3. Stage 4

    Floor tile

    Large marble-look floor tile, set flat with a leveling system.

04Floor repair

Kitchen Floor, Repaired From the Subfloor Up

A hole had broken through this kitchen’s subfloor. We took the floor down to the subfloor, repaired it, and ran new vinyl plank through the kitchen and into the eating nook.

Flooring installationKitchen improvementsHome repairsRequest a similar project

Before
A hole through the subfloor, with splintered, weakened wood around it, under several layers of old flooring.
The goal
A solid kitchen floor again, fixed at the source instead of covered over.
The work
We pulled up the old flooring layers to reach the subfloor, repaired the damaged subfloor, and installed new vinyl plank flooring.
The result
A repaired subfloor and one continuous vinyl plank floor across the kitchen and into the eating nook.
