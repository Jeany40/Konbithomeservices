Kitchen Floor, Repaired From the Subfloor Up

A hole had broken through this kitchen’s subfloor. We took the floor down to the subfloor, repaired it, and finished the kitchen and the eating nook with new vinyl plank flooring.

Flooring installationHome repairsRequest a similar project

What was damaged
A hole through the subfloor, with splintered, weakened wood around it, under several layers of old flooring.
How we fixed it
We pulled up the old flooring layers to reach the subfloor, repaired the damaged subfloor, and installed new vinyl plank flooring.
What the customer got
A repaired subfloor and one continuous new vinyl plank floor across the kitchen and the eating nook.

Basement Build-Out
