Our approach to flooring installation

A new floor is only as good as what’s under it. When we pull up an old floor, we check the subfloor, and if it’s damaged, we repair it before the new floor goes down.

Recent jobs include a kitchen where we repaired a hole through the subfloor before installing vinyl plank, a bedroom changed from stained carpet to vinyl plank, and a carpeted staircase redone with vinyl plank treads.

Typical project scope

  • Vinyl plank, laminate, hardwood, and tile floors
  • Carpet removal
  • Subfloor repairs
  • Stair treads and risers
  • New baseboard trim

Common Questions

What if my subfloor is damaged?

We repair it first. In one kitchen we found a hole through the subfloor, so we fixed the subfloor before installing the new vinyl plank floor.

Can you replace the carpet on my stairs?

Yes. We replaced worn stair carpet with vinyl plank treads and white risers. The before and after photos are on our work page.

Which flooring is right for my room?

It depends on the room and how you use it. We used vinyl plank in a recent kitchen and bedroom, and large tile in a bathroom. We’ll go over options when we give you your estimate.

Is the estimate free?

Yes. Send us a request online or call or text (718) 696-7379, and we’ll set up your free estimate.

Can I send photos of the space first?

Yes. After you send your request, text your photos to (718) 696-7379. They help us understand the job.
