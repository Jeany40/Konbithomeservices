The people behind the work

Three family members. One shared standard.

Konbit Home Services is built on family, teamwork, and pride in the work we do. Jean, Stevenson, and Mackenson bring their skills together to improve homes throughout Long Island, Queens, and Brooklyn.

Our name comes from our Haitian heritage. In Haiti, a konbit is when people come together to get a big job done, working side by side until it’s finished.

Men anpil, chay pa lou.

Many hands, light work.
