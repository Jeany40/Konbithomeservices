Our story

More Than a Crew. We’re Family.

Konbit Home Services is family-owned and operated. Jean, Mackenson, and Stevenson work as one team on every job, and we treat your home with the same care we give our own.

Our name comes from the Haitian Creole word for neighbors coming together to finish a big job. That’s how we work: many hands, one team, and attention to every detail.
