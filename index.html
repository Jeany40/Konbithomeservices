About us

More Than a Crew. We’re Family.

Konbit Home Services is a family-run company built on teamwork, trust, and craftsmanship. We treat every project like it’s our own home, with care, respect, and a commitment to quality that lasts.

Our name comes from Haitian Creole. A konbit is when family and neighbors come together to finish a big job. Jean, Stevenson, and Mackenson built this company on that idea.

Real Projects. Real Transformations.

Recent work from our crew, shown step by step.

Renovation

Tub-to-Shower Bathroom Renovation

We replaced a dated tub with a walk-in shower, finished with large marble-look wall tile, a hexagon mosaic floor, black rain and handheld shower fixtures, a built-in niche, and a sliding glass door.

How we built it

  1. Step 1

    Down to the studs

    We removed the old tub, tile, and walls down to the framing.

  2. Step 2

    A waterproof base

    New waterproof backer board on the walls, and a sealed shower base and curb around the new drain.

  3. Step 3

    Tile, set level

    Large-format wall tile installed with a leveling system, plus a recessed niche.

Built from scratch

New Bathroom, Framing to Finish

We built this bathroom from the framing up, with a tiled walk-in shower, a hexagon mosaic shower floor, a built-in niche, large marble-look floor tile, a gray shaker vanity, and a backlit mirror.

How we built it

  1. Step 1

    Walls and a waterproof base

    Waterproof backer board in the shower, mold-resistant drywall on the other walls, and a sealed shower base and curb.

  2. Step 2

    Shower tile

    Large marble-look wall tile, a hexagon mosaic floor, and a built-in niche.

  3. Step 3

    Floor tile

    Large marble-look floor tile, set flat with a leveling system.

More projects

Floors, Stairs, and Basement Work

A kitchen floor repaired from the subfloor up, a bedroom and a staircase redone in vinyl plank, and a basement build-out we’re working on now.

  • Kitchen Floor, Repaired From the Subfloor Up

    The old floor had a hole through the subfloor. We pulled up the old layers, repaired the subfloor, and installed new vinyl plank flooring.

  • Bedroom, Carpet to New Floors

    We replaced stained carpet with vinyl plank flooring, painted the walls, and added new baseboard trim.

  • Stairs, Carpet to Vinyl Treads

    We pulled up the worn carpet and installed dark vinyl plank treads with clean white risers.
